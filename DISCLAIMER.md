# Disclaimer

**TNBC-Metabolic-Strain-MOD is research software.** It is a computational / dynamical-systems model of metabolic strain in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC).

It is **not**:

- a medical device
- a diagnostic, prognostic, or companion-diagnostic product
- a clinically validated biomarker
- treatment, dosing, or patient-management guidance
- evidence that a nanobiocomposite, doxorubicin schedule, or phytochemical intervention works in people

Outputs (ATP/ROS trajectories, `G_tip`, bifurcation diagrams, Monte Carlo histograms, “treated vs control” comparisons) are **in-silico** unless a notebook explicitly loads experimental or clinical data. The notebooks currently in this repository do not.

Parameters are illustrative. Literature citations, if present, motivate modeling assumptions; they do not mean this code has reproduced or validated those studies.

Do not use this repository to make care decisions. Domain experts are invited to review the mathematics, assumptions, and limitations before any translational claim is made.
